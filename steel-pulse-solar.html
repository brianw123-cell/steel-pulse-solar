Steel Pulse Solar

Run a solar-install company. Win jobs, manage cash, build a crew, hit $1,000,000.

Realistic

Real margins, tighter cash. Learn the business.

Beginner

Gentler money, plain-language coaching.

How to play
  • Each turn is one week. Accept jobs, assign nothing (your crew auto-works the active jobs), then tap Move forward one week.
  • Watch cash, not just sales. You pay for panels up front and collect a deposit, then get the rest when the job finishes.
  • Staff each job with about 3-4 crew. Too few and quality drops and jobs crawl, too many and you pay idle hands. More trucks let you run more jobs at once.
  • Goal: grow net worth to $1,000,000. Hit $0 cash with no credit left and you go bankrupt.
